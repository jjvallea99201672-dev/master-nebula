II.

Grammar Rules (9)

1
Possession
Unmarked juxtaposition is default: eya luma (sky-light). Particle mei is optional and postposed: NOUN + mei + NOUN.
eya luma · ira mei sola
2
Predication
Copula va is optional. Bare juxtaposition predicates: sola ari = peace comes.
sola va ari · sola ari
3
Subject Ellipsis
Subjects are droppable when recoverable from context or verb semantics. Lyric voice favors ellipsis.
(ni) sen · (sa) rei
4
Tense
Present is unmarked. Pre-verbal particles: nu past, fa future.
sa ari · nu sa ari · fa sa ari
5
Negation
no appears immediately pre-element (verb/adjective). In cluster: tense → negator → verb.
no ari · fa ni no sen
6
Adjectives
Any content root can modify by juxtaposition. Position: after noun or predicative. ta immediately pre-adjectival intensifies.
kele sina · ta sina kele · ta vey tala
7
Coordination
e = and, placed between conjuncts. No comma. Stackable for litany.
ari e sen e sai
8
Numerals
Numerals precede noun. No agreement or plural marking.
du tala · tri yola · u ori
9
Particle Ordering
Strict: [ke] → [tense nu/fa] → [pronoun]* → [neg no] → VERB → [object] → [ya]. ta + ADJ is a tight unit. Deictics nai/na float. *Pronouns may float between tense and negator (e.g., fa ni no sen) or be elided.
ke nu no ari ya · fa no sera ya

if sky shine, peace come

= Very steady stone, very long road.

V.

Open Edges — Intentionally Unclosed

Interrogatives
open
No wh-words. Questions inferred by intonation / context. Lyric may use rising final na.
Comparatives
open
No morphological comparative. Comparison is approximated via periphrasis or juxtaposition of qualities.
Temporal 'when'
open
No when. Use ke (if) + tense or bare juxtaposition of events.
Causal 'because'
open
No because. Parataxis: sera, sola ari — it shines, peace comes.
Plural
context-driven
No plural marking. oma / pai or numerals disambiguate. du tala = two stars, tala = star(s).
Deixis nai
overloaded by design
nai = now/here. Spatial vs temporal intentional blur. na is line-final poetic variant.
Relative Clauses
unmarked juxtaposition
No relativizer. Noun + clause modifies: tala nu sa sera — star that shone.
