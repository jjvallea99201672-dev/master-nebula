Phonology Rules

  • Open syllables preferred: CV, CVV, CVC
  • Allowed word endings: vowel, -n, -l, -m, -ra
  • Soft consonants: l, n, r, v, th, sh, m, s
  • No hard consonant clusters
  • Diacritics mark sustained vowels: ā, ē, ī

Grammar

Tense

TenseMarkerExample
Present-rei or -a suffixluma-a = is shining
Past-en suffixluma-en = shone
Futureva- prefixva-luma = will shine
Continuous/Eternal-ai / -thae suffixelai = ever-calling

Negation

Place na before the verb:

  • luma = shine
  • na luma = does not shine
  • nae vae = never come
  • na thira = no end (from your bridge)

Questions

Add lo at end, voice rises:

  • alei vae lo? = Do you call?
  • ma ora lo? = Who speaks?

Word Order

Subject — Verb — Object Descriptors follow what they modify:

  • mira sol = light-source = sunlight
  • saela oru nai = seven voices unified
