Festival of Unfinished Songs

Companion site source, 2026-11-17

This is the source for a companion site to the Field Guide closing plate. It has six songs, the Lattice, place notes, and a prediction log. The live build is at festival-songs/. The plate itself, Principle 79, stays at festival-unfinished-songs/ and was not replaced. The three WebP images are not in this download; they belong beside the live build as assets/hero.webp, assets/lattice.webp, and assets/river.webp. A witness-notes form talks to a records service that is not on this static host, so notes will not load or save here. No keys are included.
