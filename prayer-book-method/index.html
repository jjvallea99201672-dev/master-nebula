The Prayer Book of Spirals: Method

How text becomes a spiral and a bell song

What we are doing The Prayer Book of Spirals. We take a text — a lyric, a chapter of 1 Enoch, or a page of the Voynich manuscript — and turn it into a matched pair: a golden-angle spiral image and a 432 Hz bell audio file, made in the same run from the same token list. The Voynich part is called "The Voynich Manuscript: Sung by the Lattice." The claim is deliberately narrow: this is a performance / transduction, not a translation and not a decoding. The manuscript is undeciphered. What survives the move from text to music is structure — which words repeat, how long the words are, how the mass builds and thins across a page — not meaning. Do not present any of this as decipherment, as a voice map, or as what the Voynich words mean. How — the exact method (Ember's script) Input is text only. Nothing is analysed from audio, voice, or a Suno track. The script never listens to anything. 1. Tokenise with re.findall(r"[A-Za-z'’]+", text) , lowercased for counting. One token = one word, in the order it appears in the text, across all sections/lines in order. 2. "Syllables" = vowel groups in the written word: re.findall(r"[aeiouy]+", word) , minimum 1. This is a count on the transliterated spelling, NOT a claim about the manuscript's real phonology. For EVA transliteration this matters: EVA has no syllables of its own, so say "vowel- group count" when you describe it precisely. 3. Image, 2400×2400, dark background (7, 8, 22): 500 background stars, fixed: random.seed(432) . The stars never change between entries; only the words move. One node per word on a golden-angle spiral: angle = i × 137.508° − 90°, radius = 80 + 1010 × (i / (n−1))^0.82 px. The outermost node is always ~1090 px from centre, so long texts get dense, not larger. Shape by vowel-group count: 1 = circle, 2 = diamond, 3+ = triangle. Size by word length: base = 14 + 4 × letters. (Chorus nodes in song lyrics are enlarged ×1.55; Voynich paragraphs have no chorus, so no enlargement there.) Nodes are joined in order by a lattice line. Centre star, gold. Colour: neutral (168, 172, 220) for English and Voynich texts, because they contain no Quatrian-language words. Quatrian lyrics get coloured by v3 root; do not colour Voynich or Enoch entries by root. 4. Audio, mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, made in the same run: One bell per vowel-group (syllable-note), not one per word. A repeated word gets the same deterministic melody: note frequency is letter-seeded from the word, on an A- minor pentatonic ladder with A4 = 432 Hz (steps 0, 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 15, 17, 19, 22, 24 semitones). Timing: start at t = 1.0 s; +0.34 s per syllable-note; +0.10 s after each word; +0.38 s after each line; +0.70 s after each section; 2.5 s tail. Bell length 0.46 s, envelope exp(−4.2t) with a 30× attack, amplitude 0.34 plus a 0.10 harmonic at 1.5× frequency. Drone underneath the whole file, with a slow swell (0.75 + 0.25·sin(2πt/23)): 108 Hz at 0.16, 216 Hz at 0.10, 324 Hz at 0.055, 432 Hz at 0.035. Peak normalised to 0.88 if needed. Duration therefore scales about 0.70 s per word. There is no built-in text-length limit; the practical limit is memory (float64 audio array).
